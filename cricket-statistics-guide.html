Understanding Cricket Statistics: A Complete Guide for Beginners

Cricket is a sport full of numbers. From runs and wickets to batting averages, strike rates and bowling economy rates, statistics help explain how a player or team has performed.

For someone who is new to cricket, these numbers can sometimes look confusing. Understanding the most common statistics makes it easier to follow matches, compare performances and understand scorecards.
